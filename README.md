# Magic Cave Escape (Name in Progress)

## Vision Statement

Magic Cave Escape is a short magical cave exploration game where the player is transported underground and must find a way out. The game will focus on simple combat, exploration, and a small number of magical abilities.

## Gameplay

The player will explore a dark magical cave, fight enemies, and use simple elemental abilities to progress through the level.

The game will be designed as a short playable experience rather than a full-length game. The main goal is to create one complete cave area with working movement, combat, enemies, and at least one or two magical rune abilities.

### Mechanics

The main mechanics of the game will include:

- Walking
- Jumping
- Picking up or equipping items
- Sword attacks
- Strong sword attack by holding the attack button
- Using magical rune abilities
- Sprinting or dashing with the Air Rune
- Using an updraft with the Air Rune

The player will begin with a sword for basic combat.

The game may include elemental runes that give the player different abilities. The four planned elements are fire, water, earth, and air, although not all of them may be fully implemented during the project.

Possible rune abilities include:

- **Fire:** Used mainly for attacking enemies.
- **Water:** Used for simple environmental puzzles.
- **Earth:** Used defensively, such as creating a shield.
- **Air:** Used for movement, such as dashing or creating an updraft.

For the prototype, the focus will be on implementing the abilities that are most useful to the level design and are realistic to complete within the project timeline.

Enemies will have simple attack patterns. Some enemies may use projectiles to create light bullet-hell style combat, but this will depend on development time.

### Scoring and/or Win / Lose Conditions

The player wins by reaching the end of the cave and finding or activating the exit portal.

The player has health and loses health when hit by enemies or hazards.

If the player's health reaches zero, they lose and restart from the beginning of the level or from a checkpoint.

Enemies may drop health when defeated.

The game will not use a traditional score system. Progress will mainly be shown by how far the player has traveled through the cave and whether they have reached the exit.

### Controls

Possible controls include:

- **WASD / Arrow Keys:** Move
- **Space:** Jump
- **Shift:** Sprint or dash when the Air Rune is equipped
- **Attack Button:** Sword attack
- **Hold Attack Button:** Strong sword attack
- **Magic Button:** Use the currently equipped rune ability
- **Interact Button:** Pick up items or activate objects

The final controls may change depending on how the game feels during development.

### Aesthetic

The game will use a dark magical cave aesthetic inspired by the underground Hallow areas in Terraria.

The cave will mostly be dark, with glowing crystals, magical objects, runes, and sprites providing the main sources of light.

A glowing sprite will follow the player and act as a light source.

The environment should feel mysterious and magical without requiring a large amount of detailed artwork.

The game will likely use a limited number of cave tiles, objects, and effects so that the team can focus on creating a complete playable level.

### Desired Player Experience

The player should feel curious while exploring the cave and slightly unsure of what they will find next.

The game should feel easy to understand but still give the player moments where they need to react to enemies or figure out how to use their abilities.

The main goal is for the player to have a short but complete experience where they enter the cave, learn the game's mechanics, fight enemies, and successfully escape.

## Game Characters

The main character is the player character who is transported into the underground cave.

The player begins with a sword and later discovers magical abilities through runes.

A small glowing sprite follows the player. Its main purpose is to provide light in dark areas and make the environment easier to see.

The game will also include a small number of enemy types.

Enemies may have different movement or attack patterns, but the number of enemies will be kept limited so that each one can be completed within the project timeline.

## Story

The game begins with the player above ground.

The player discovers a mysterious portal and is transported into an underground magical cave.

After waking up, the player realizes they are trapped underground.

A glowing sprite begins following the player and provides light while they explore.

The player finds a sword and uses it to defend themselves from creatures inside the cave.

While exploring, the player may also discover magical runes that give them elemental abilities.

The player's main goal is to travel through the cave and find another portal that allows them to escape.

The story will be kept simple so that the project can focus mainly on gameplay.

## The Game World

The game will take place inside one main underground cave area.

The cave will contain different sections that introduce combat, platforming, and possibly simple puzzles.

The level may include small visual changes to make different areas feel unique, but the game will not require multiple large worlds.

Possible areas include:

- Starting area where the player wakes up
- Basic combat area
- Rune or ability area
- Platforming section
- Final area with the exit portal

The cave may include glowing crystals, magical plants, rocks, platforms, water, and other simple environmental objects.

Because the game takes place underground, there will not be a normal day and night cycle or weather system.

Lighting will mainly come from the player's sprite and glowing objects in the cave.
